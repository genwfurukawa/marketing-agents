# Mining reviews and support

Objections in the buyer's own words, already written down, already public. This is
the cheapest input in the system and the most commonly skipped.

## Where to look

| Source | Gives you |
|---|---|
| G2, Capterra, TrustRadius | Structured comparison language, and why they chose a rival |
| Reddit and niche communities | What they say when no vendor is in the room |
| Your support queue | Where the product confuses people, which is where content is missing |
| Competitor reviews | The objection you can answer and they cannot |

## What to extract

**Read competitor reviews more carefully than your own.** A three star review of a
rival is a list of the promises the category makes and breaks. That is where
positioning lives.

For each source, collect:
1. The exact phrases people use for the problem, before any vendor named it
2. The comparison sets they consider, which are rarely the ones you assume
3. The switching triggers, which is the "why now" you cannot invent
4. The praise that is specific, which is usually more useful than the criticism

## The rule

**Copy phrasing, never invent it.** If buyers say "it takes three weeks to get a
campaign out," that is the sentence. Rewriting it as "protracted campaign cycles"
is how a company ends up sounding like every other company in its category.

Phrases go to `BRAND.md`. Objections go to `CONTEXT.md`. Comparison sets go to
`ICP.md`.
