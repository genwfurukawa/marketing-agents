# The founder interview

One session, 60 to 90 minutes, recorded. The goal is not a transcript. It is the
handful of things this person knows that are nowhere on the website.

## Before you start

Read everything they have already published. Arriving with "so what do you do" wastes
the only hour you get and tells them you did not prepare.

## The questions that work

**On the category**
1. What do most people get wrong about this problem?
2. What did you believe three years ago that you no longer believe?
3. What does your best competitor understand that the rest of the market does not?

**On the buyer**
4. Walk me through the last deal you won. What was the moment it turned?
5. What is the objection your reps answer most often, and what actually resolves it?
6. Who should not buy this, and how do you tell early?

**On proof**
7. What claim do you most want to make but cannot back up yet?
8. What number surprised you when you first measured it?
9. What have you done for a customer that you have never written down?

**On the work**
10. What is the campaign you keep meaning to run and keep pushing?
11. What is in market right now that you are not proud of, and why is it still up?

## The rule

**Chase the stories, not the answers.** When they say "our customers usually
struggle with onboarding," the useful material is one level down: which customer,
what happened, what did they say. Ask "can you give me a specific example" every
time. The generality is what everyone already has.

## What comes out

Answers to 1, 2, 3 and 7 become POV in `CONTEXT.md`. Answers to 4, 5 and 6 become
`ICP.md`. Answer 9 is usually the best case study nobody has written. The recording
itself is the first input to `voice-extract.md`.
