# THE BRAIN

The substrate at the centre of the loop. Every stage reads from it before it does
anything. If the Brain is wrong, everything downstream is confidently wrong.

**This is the step every other vendor skips.** It is why their output sounds like
nobody in particular.

## The three files

| File | Holds | Built from |
|---|---|---|
| `CONTEXT.md` | What you sell, who buys it, what you can prove | Interviews, sales calls, support tickets, reviews |
| `BRAND.md` | How you sound when you are writing well | Your own best existing work. Never invented. |
| `ICP.md` | Who you are for, and who you disqualify | Closed-won patterns, closed-lost reasons |

Together these are **the Brain** and **the Voice**, two of the four artifacts
that carry state through the loop. They are the asset. Everything
downstream is only as good as they are.

## Building them

The raw material already exists. It is in a founder's head, in a recording nobody
opened, in last quarter's sales calls, in the support queue, in your reviews.

`capture/` holds the interview guides and extraction prompts that get it out:

- `capture/founder-interview.md`: the questions that surface what only they know
- `capture/call-mining.md`: reading sales calls for objections and buyer language
- `capture/review-mining.md`: G2, Reddit and support tickets in the buyer's words
- `capture/voice-extract.md`: deriving BRAND.md from work you already published

**Raw material is not leverage. Encoded material is.**

## Every fact carries its source

A Brain entry without a `source:` line pointing at something real (a
transcript, a review, a page you can open) is worse than no entry at all,
because the Source gate will resolve it and pass. CAPTURE is not done until
every entry has one. Record the verbatim quote, not your tightening of it:
the Fabrication gate compares draft text against what is stored here, so a
paraphrase in the Brain defeats the check downstream.

## The judgment that stays human

When a new signal arrives, is it a *topic* or a *truth*? A recurring objection might
be a blog post, or it might mean the positioning is wrong. Getting that wrong is how
a system produces a year of well made, off target work.

Topics go to a loop. Truths change the Brain. Do not let an agent make that call.

## Keeping it current

The Brain is not set once. Every approved piece and every rejection is a signal about
what is right. Month two sounds more like you than month one because the system
watched what you approved.
