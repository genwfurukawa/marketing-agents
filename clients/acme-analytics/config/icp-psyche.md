# Acme Analytics — ICP Psyche (DEMO)

Fictional demo profile. Replace with your real ICP research.

## Primary ICP

Head of Product or Senior PM at a B2B SaaS company, Series A-C, 50-500 employees.
Owns activation and retention metrics. Technical enough to write a SQL query under
duress but doesn't want to. Reports to a VP Product or CPO.

## Emotional Landscape

Stretched thin. Accountable for retention numbers they can't fully explain. Quietly
anxious that the data team views their questions as noise. Ambitious — wants to be
seen as the PM who ships with evidence, not opinions.

## Exact Buyer Language

- "I can see that activation dropped, I just can't see why."
- "Every analytics question is a two-week ticket."
- "Our Mixpanel bill is a mystery every month."
- "I don't want another dashboard nobody looks at."

## Buying Anxiety

Switching cost (re-instrumentation is painful), fear of another unpredictable
per-event bill, worry that the data team will veto the tool, reputational risk if a
new tool's numbers disagree with the old one.

## FOMO Triggers

Competitors shipping faster because their PMs self-serve. Peers at other companies
talking about "answering questions in minutes." A board that expects data-backed
product decisions.

## Buying Triggers

A churn spike nobody can explain. A new VP Product who demands evidence. A surprise
analytics bill. The data team explicitly asking PMs to stop filing ad-hoc requests.

## Objections

- "We already have Amplitude." → Real concern: sunk cost + re-instrumentation pain.
- "Our data team owns analytics." → Real concern: internal politics, not capability.
- "Per-event pricing scares me." → Real concern: unpredictable spend at scale.
