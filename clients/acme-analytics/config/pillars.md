# Acme Analytics — Content Pillars (DEMO)

Fictional demo pillars. Replace with your own.

## Pillar 1: Self-Serve Analytics

- **Role:** Authority + demand. The contrarian flagship.
- **Content %:** 35%
- **POV:** Every PM should answer their own product questions in minutes. The data
  team is for hard problems, not "what was activation last week".
- **Channels:** LinkedIn, blog, email
- **Seed keywords:** self-serve product analytics, PM analytics, analytics without SQL

## Pillar 2: Why Not What (Behavioral Insight)

- **Role:** Awareness + education.
- **Content %:** 25%
- **POV:** Dashboards tell you what happened. Retention is won by understanding why.
- **Channels:** LinkedIn, blog
- **Seed keywords:** why users churn, behavioral product analytics, retention analysis

## Pillar 3: Pricing & Predictability

- **Role:** Demand + bottom-funnel. Targets the per-event-pricing pain.
- **Content %:** 20%
- **POV:** Per-event pricing punishes the teams that instrument well. Flat pricing
  aligns the tool with the job.
- **Channels:** Blog, email, comparison pages
- **Seed keywords:** product analytics pricing, Amplitude pricing, Mixpanel cost

## Pillar 4: Instrumentation & Migration

- **Role:** Retention + activation for new users; reduces switching anxiety.
- **Content %:** 20%
- **POV:** Migration doesn't have to mean re-instrumenting from scratch.
- **Channels:** Blog, docs, integration pages
- **Seed keywords:** analytics migration, event tracking setup, instrumentation guide

## Weekly Calendar

Mon: Self-Serve pillar (LinkedIn). Wed: Why-Not-What (blog). Fri: Pricing or
Migration (comparison/integration page).

## Content-to-Revenue Map

- Top of funnel: Why Not What, Self-Serve
- Mid: Pricing & Predictability (comparisons, alternatives)
- Bottom: Migration (integration guides, case studies)
