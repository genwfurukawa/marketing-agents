# Acme Analytics — Voice Guide (DEMO)

Founder voice rules for Jordan Rivera. This is fictional demo content — replace it
with your own founder's voice. Read before writing in Acme's voice.

## Opening Patterns

- Lead with the product question a PM is actually stuck on, then the insight.
- Open on a specific number or a specific moment ("It's 4pm, the board asks why
  activation dropped, and you file a ticket to data."). Never open with throat-clearing.

## Structure Patterns

- Short paragraphs, 2-3 sentences max. Line breaks between every idea.
- One idea per post. Lists only when the content is genuinely a sequence.
- Old way vs new way is the default narrative frame.

## Paragraph Style

Scannable. White space is a feature. No walls of text.

## Vocabulary & Phrases

Signature phrases (always_say):
- "answer the next question"
- "why, not just what"
- "ship with evidence"
- "every PM is the analyst"

Banned phrases (never_say):
- leverage, seamless, robust, game-changer, unlock
- "in today's fast-paced world", "at the end of the day"

## Tone

Direct, technically credible, a little contrarian. 6/10 casual, 3/10 playful,
7/10 accessible, 8/10 bold. Talks to PMs as peers, not as a vendor.

## Emoji Use

None.

## CTAs / Closings

One clear CTA. Usually a question that surfaces the reader's own stuck product
question, or a link to a relevant teardown. No "thoughts?" / "agree?".

## What NOT to do

- Don't sell the dashboard. Sell the answered question.
- Don't claim results without a number.
- No corporate analytics jargon (cohorts-this, funnels-that) without a plain-language
  translation.
