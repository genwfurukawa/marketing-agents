# Step 2: Positioning + POV

> **Question**: What do you believe that others do not?

## Pipeline Position
- **Receives from**: Step 1 (ICP-validated topic with buyer context)
- **Produces for**: Step 3 (Opinionated angle with contrarian take)
- **Agent**: `.claude/agents/positioning-agent.md`
- **Schema**: `schemas/positioning_input.json` -> `schemas/positioning_output.json`

## Purpose
Define your unique point of view and what makes you different. This prevents "correct but invisible" content that could be swapped with a competitor.

## Subfolders

| Folder | What Goes Here |
|--------|---------------|
| `pov_library/` | Core beliefs, contrarian takes, strong opinions |
| `positioning_statements/` | How we're different, value propositions |
| `category_narratives/` | The story of how the category should work |
| `what_we_refuse/` | Topics, approaches, statements we won't make |

## Checklist
- [ ] Clear beliefs about how the category should work
- [ ] Clear disagreements with common industry narratives
- [ ] A category story your team can articulate consistently
- [ ] What you refuse to say

## Failure Signal
Your content is correct, but invisible. Safe thought leadership. Content that could be swapped with a competitor.

## Output
POV library and positioning statements that inform all content creation.
