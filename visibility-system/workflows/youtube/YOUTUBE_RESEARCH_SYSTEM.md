## Data Collection Framework

### Step 1: Channel-Level Metrics

For each channel above, collect:
- [ ] Total subscribers
- [ ] Average views per video (last 30 videos)
- [ ] Upload frequency
- [ ] Subscriber-to-view ratio (engagement indicator)
- [ ] Content format breakdown (tutorials/news/reviews/etc.)

### Step 2: Video-Level Performance Analysis

For top 20 videos on each channel:
- [ ] Title structure and keywords
- [ ] View count
- [ ] Upload date (to calculate views over time)
- [ ] Video length
- [ ] Thumbnail style (categorize common patterns)
- [ ] Topic/category
- [ ] Views-to-subscriber ratio (% of audience who watched)

### Step 3: Topic Validation

Cross-reference our 15 video ideas against top-performing competitor topics:
- [ ] Is anyone covering this topic? (If no, why not? Opportunity or non-viable?)
- [ ] What's the average view count for similar topics?
- [ ] How are they titling/packaging similar concepts?
- [ ] What angles are they missing that we could own?

## Tools to Use

### Option 1: VidIQ (Recommended)
**Pricing:** Free plan available, Pro at $39/month

**What it provides:**
- Competitor channel analytics
- Keyword research for YouTube
- Tag analysis
- Thumbnail A/B testing insights
- Trend alerts in your niche

**How to use:**
1. Install VidIQ browser extension
2. Add competitor channels to "Competitors" list
3. Export channel analytics CSV
4. Use keyword research for title validation

**Best for:** Understanding what keywords/topics are searchable and trending

### Option 2: TubeBuddy
**Pricing:** Free plan available, Pro at $9/month

**What it provides:**
- A/B testing for thumbnails and titles
- SEO optimization suggestions
- Competitor scorecard
- Bulk processing tools

**How to use:**
1. Install TubeBuddy extension
2. Use "Competitor Analysis" feature
3. Export data for competitor channels

**Best for:** Optimizing individual videos and testing variations

### Option 3: Manual Research (Free, But Time-Consuming)

**Process:**
1. Open each competitor channel in incognito mode
2. Sort by "Most Popular" videos
3. Manually record: Title, Views, Upload Date, Length, Topic
4. Create spreadsheet for analysis
5. Look for patterns in high-performing videos

**Best for:** Starting immediately without tool investment

### Option 4: YouTube Analytics API (Advanced)
**Pricing:** Free (Google Cloud)

**What it provides:**
- Programmatic access to public YouTube data
- Can automate data collection
- Build custom dashboards

**How to use:**
1. Set up Google Cloud project
2. Enable YouTube Data API v3
3. Use Claude Code to write data collection scripts
4. Store in Notion for ongoing tracking

**Best for:** Ongoing competitive intelligence automation

## Analysis Workflow

### Weekly Competitive Research (30 mins)

1. **Scan Competitor Uploads**
   - What did top 5 competitors publish this week?
   - Which videos are getting early traction (high views in first 48 hours)?
   - Any new topic trends emerging?

2. **Update Topic Opportunity Score**
   - Cross-reference our idea backlog with what's working
   - Identify gaps competitors aren't covering
   - Flag time-sensitive opportunities (news, tool launches)

3. **Thumbnail & Title Analysis**
   - Screenshot top performers
   - Note common patterns (color schemes, text, expressions)
   - Test hypotheses with our own designs

### Monthly Deep Dive (2 hours)

1. **Channel Performance Review**
   - Which channels grew fastest this month?
   - What content drove that growth?
   - Any format shifts (e.g., more Shorts, longer videos)?

2. **Topic Clustering**
   - Group competitor content into categories
   - Identify over-saturated topics (avoid)
   - Find under-served topics (opportunity)

3. **Update Video Idea Backlog**
   - Validate our ideas with actual performance data
   - Re-score ideas based on competitive landscape
   - Add new ideas inspired by gap analysis

### Quarterly Strategy Adjustment (4 hours)

1. **Content Mix Optimization**
   - Which format performs best? (Tutorials vs. Walkthroughs vs. Build-in-Public)
   - Should we adjust our 40/30/20/10 split?
   - Are we differentiated enough from competitors?

2. **SEO vs. Algorithm Balance**
   - How much traffic is coming from search vs. suggested?
   - Should we shift SEO-first strategy based on data?

3. **Lead Generation Correlation**
   - Which video topics drove discovery calls?
   - Can we identify patterns in "high-conversion" topics?

## How to Give More Context on Your ICP & Channel Goals

### Update Your Client Profile

Edit: `/{client_root}/LIGHTHOUSE_CLIENT_PROFILE.md`

Add sections:
- **YouTube-Specific Goals:** (What do you want viewers to do after watching?)
- **Content Preferences:** (What your ICP watches on YouTube vs. LinkedIn)
- **Competitor Channels They Follow:** (Who are they already learning from?)
- **Pain Points Unique to Video:** (Different from written content consumption)

### Create a Channel Strategy Doc

Create: `/{client_root}/workflows/youtube/CHANNEL_STRATEGY.md`

Include:
- **Channel positioning statement:** (How you want to be known)
- **Content pillars:** (3-5 core themes you'll always cover)
- **Voice & tone guidelines:** (Speaking style for videos)
- **Topics to avoid:** (What you won't cover, even if it gets views)
- **Success definition:** (Beyond views - what matters most?)

### Feed Context to Agents

When running YouTube agents, provide additional context:

**Example:**
```
/youtube ideas "AEO tutorials for marketing directors"

Additional context:
- Our ICP is risk-averse, data-driven marketing directors
- They prefer tactical how-tos over theoretical frameworks
- They watch YouTube during lunch breaks (prefer 10-15 min videos)
- They need content they can show their CEO to justify budgets
- They follow Ahrefs, HubSpot, and Neil Patel currently
```

### Iterate Based on Analytics

After publishing first 5-10 videos:
1. Review YouTube Studio Analytics
2. Identify which topics/formats performed best
3. Survey your viewers (community tab poll)
4. Update your ICP profile with actual data
5. Re-run video ideation with new insights

## Questions to Answer About Your ICP (For Better Ideas)

1. **Content Consumption Habits:**
   - Do they watch YouTube during work hours or personal time?
   - Do they prefer watching or reading?
   - How long will they invest in a tutorial?

2. **Decision-Making Process:**
   - Are they looking for quick wins or strategic frameworks?
   - Do they need ammunition to convince their CEO?
   - Are they hands-on (will implement) or delegators (will assign to team)?

3. **Current Knowledge Level:**
   - How much do they already know about AEO?
   - Are they AI-native or AI-curious?
   - Do we need to explain basics or can we go deep?

4. **Channel Discovery:**
   - How will they find your channel? (Search, LinkedIn shares, recommendations?)
   - What would make them subscribe after one video?
   - What would make them return for video #2?

5. **Competition Awareness:**
   - Which YouTube channels do they currently follow for marketing education?
   - Why do they watch those channels vs. others?
   - What are we offering that those channels aren't?
