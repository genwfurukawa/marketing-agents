# sm-social

Owned distribution, and the signal it returns.

## A channel is a port, not a stage

The valuable thing about social is not that you publish there. It is that it runs in
both directions. What you publish generates tomorrow's CAPTURE input: who engaged, what
they said, which topic drew buyers rather than peers.

That loop back is the cheapest test available, and most teams never wire it because
they treat publishing as the end of the process rather than the middle.

## IMPROVE feeds CAPTURE

The number that matters is not reach. It is which topics produced people who look
like your buyer. A post with a hundred thousand views and no buyers in the comments
underperformed a post with four hundred views and two.

Route that finding to `sm-content` CAPTURE weekly. It is the highest signal input
that loop receives.
