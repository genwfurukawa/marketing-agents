# sm-demand

Turning attention into named, scored people. This loop is mostly judgment, which is
why it is mostly L1.

## What is here and what is not

**Not here: any scraper.** That is deliberate and worth explaining rather than
leaving as a gap.

Extraction is commodity. Every platform prohibits automated collection in its terms,
and while public data scraping has survived court challenge as a computer fraud
question, a platform can still restrict your account for breach of terms. Putting
your primary channel at risk to obtain data a licensed tool already sells you is a
bad trade.

**Here: the scoring layer.** The judgment on top of the data is the only part worth
owning, and it is the part nobody publishes.

Use a licensed tool for collection, or collect manually. Manual collection produces
better signal than people expect, because reading the thread is the work.

## Mine comments, not likes

A like costs nothing. A comment is stated intent, in their words, with a timestamp.

**Mine by topic, not by author.** Someone who commented "we are frozen on headcount
and the board keeps asking about AI" is a perfect prospect regardless of whose post
they said it under. Watching your competitors' followers is lower yield than it
sounds, because most of them are that competitor's customers and staff.

Highest yield: pain posts tracked by keyword, category creators whose audience is
your buyer, and your own posts.

## The scoring rubric

Score 0 to 100. Touch at 70 or above.

| Factor | Weight | Scoring |
|---|---|---|
| Title fit | 30 | Target title 30, adjacent senior 22, manager 12, other 0 |
| Company fit | 25 | Core segment 25, adjacent 15, other B2B 6, agency 0 |
| Engagement depth | 25 | Substantive comment 25, repost with note 18, short comment 15, like 3 |
| Trigger present | 15 | Unfilled req 60+ days, recent funding, freeze signal |
| Recency | 5 | Within 7 days 5, 8 to 30 days 2, older 0 |

**Anti targets score zero regardless of the other factors.** In our case that is
agencies, who engage constantly and buy never. Set yours explicitly or the rubric
will happily rank your loudest non buyers at the top.

## The rule that makes it work

**Never say "I saw you engaged with X's post."** That is the tell of automation and
it announces the person was harvested.

**Comment first, then message.** Reply substantively in public under the original
post. A day or two later, the message references the conversation you actually had,
because you had it. Ten minutes a day, and it doubles as your daily comment quota.

It is the honest version, and it is also the one that works.

## Weekly runbook

- **Monday, 40 minutes.** Pull last week's engagers, score, take the top 40, split
  into *touch now* and *engage first*.
- **Daily, 25 minutes.** Comment on five threads. Message the ones you commented
  alongside 24 to 48 hours ago.
- **Friday, 20 minutes.** Reply rate by source post. Which topics produced buyers
  rather than peers. That number goes to `sm-content` CAPTURE.
