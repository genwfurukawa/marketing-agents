## SECTION 09: Banned Words & Phrases

<!-- INSTRUCTIONS: This is the comprehensive AI slop detection and prevention list.
Every word below makes AI content sound like AI content. Ban them all.
Add brand-specific bans at the bottom. Be aggressive - it's easier to unban a word than to catch AI slop.
Maps to: constraints/banned_words.md

RULE OF THUMB: If a word makes your content sound like it could have been written by any brand,
for any audience, at any time - ban it. -->

### 9.1 AI Slop Verbs (Never Use)

These are the verbs AI reaches for by default. Every single one screams "a machine wrote this."

| Banned Word | Why It's Banned | Use Instead |
|---|---|---|
| Leverage | AI's #1 favorite word | Use, apply, build on |
| Utilize | Nobody talks like this | Use |
| Delve | Instant AI detection | Explore, dig into, look at |
| Navigate | Overused metaphor | Handle, work through, figure out |
| Foster | Sounds like a textbook | Build, grow, encourage |
| Harness | Corporate speak | Use, tap into, put to work |
| Streamline | Vague and overused | Simplify, speed up, cut |
| Optimize | Use sparingly if at all | Improve, fine-tune, fix |
| Facilitate | Nobody says this | Help, enable, make possible |
| Spearhead | Unnecessarily dramatic | Lead, run, drive |
| Empower | Vague feel-good verb | Help, give, enable |
| Elevate | AI loves this metaphor | Improve, raise, strengthen |
| Unlock | Nothing is literally locked | Get, access, open up |
| Amplify | Marketing buzzword | Increase, grow, spread |
| Supercharge | Hyperbolic and cringey | Speed up, boost, improve |
| Revolutionize | Almost never accurate | Change, rethink, reshape |
| Transform | Overused when vague | Change, shift, rebuild (be specific) |
| Craft | AI's favorite "creative" verb | Write, build, create, make |
| Curate | Pretentious for "pick" | Pick, choose, collect, gather |
| Unpack | Overused analysis metaphor | Break down, explain, look at |
| Underscore | Formal and stiff | Highlight, show, prove |
| Bolster | Nobody uses this in real life | Strengthen, support, back up |
| Catalyze | Science-class jargon | Start, spark, kick off |
| Reimagine | Consultant-speak | Rethink, redesign, redo |
| Pivot | Overused corporate verb | Shift, change direction, switch |

### 9.2 AI Slop Adjectives (Never Use)

These adjectives add zero meaning. They're the verbal equivalent of clip art.

| Banned Word | Why It's Banned | Use Instead |
|---|---|---|
| Robust | AI's favorite adjective | Strong, reliable, thorough |
| Seamless / Seamlessly | Nothing is seamless | Smooth, easy, simple |
| Holistic | Vague and overused | Complete, full, end-to-end |
| Cutting-edge | Vague claim | Name the specific technology |
| State-of-the-art | Meaningless superlative | Describe what makes it new |
| Best-in-class | Everyone claims this | Show with data instead |
| Groundbreaking | Almost never true | New, first, different - with proof |
| Innovative | Emptiest word in tech | Describe the actual innovation |
| Dynamic | Means nothing specific | Flexible, fast-moving, changing |
| Scalable | Overused when vague | Grows with you, handles 10x load |
| Transformative | Adjective form of "transform" | Be specific about the change |
| Unparalleled | Hyperbolic and unprovable | Leading, top-performing, ahead of |
| Actionable | Overused in content marketing | Practical, specific, ready to use |
| Impactful | Vague buzzword | Effective, meaningful, measurable |
| Nuanced | AI uses this to sound smart | Detailed, specific, layered |
| Bespoke | Pretentious for "custom" | Custom, tailored, built for you |
| Myriad | Nobody says this out loud | Many, a range of, dozens of |
| Multifaceted | Academic filler | Complex, layered, has many parts |
| Comprehensive | AI loves this word | Be specific about scope |
| Tailored | Overused in SaaS copy | Built for, designed for, fits |

### 9.3 Empty Intensifiers & Fillers (Never Use)

These words pretend to add emphasis but actually weaken your writing.

| Banned Word | Why It's Banned | Use Instead |
|---|---|---|
| Very | Weakens whatever follows | Cut entirely or use a stronger word |
| Really | Adds nothing | Cut entirely |
| Extremely | Hyperbolic filler | Cut or quantify |
| Incredibly | Hollow emphasis | Cut or be specific |
| Significantly | Vague scale | Quantify: 3x, 40%, doubled |
| Fundamentally | Pretentious filler | Cut entirely |
| Essentially | Stalling word | Cut entirely |
| Arguably | Hedging word | State the claim directly |
| Literally | Usually not literal | Cut entirely |
| Absolutely | Filler enthusiasm | Cut entirely - your point should stand on its own |

### 9.4 Buzzwords & Jargon (Never Use)

Corporate jargon that makes content sound like it was generated for a board meeting.

| Banned Word | Why It's Banned | Use Instead |
|---|---|---|
| Synergy | Peak corporate cringe | Collaboration, working together |
| Ecosystem | Overused in tech | System, network, community |
| Paradigm shift | Nobody talks like this | Big change, new approach |
| Game-changer | Hyperbolic and generic | Be specific about the impact |

### 9.5 Corporate Filler Phrases (Never Use)

The throat-clearing that AI does before getting to the point.

| Banned Phrase | Why It's Banned | Use Instead |
|---|---|---|
| In today's fast-paced world | The #1 AI cliche opener | Start with a specific fact or story |
| In today's digital landscape | Equally generic | Name the actual trend or change |
| In the ever-evolving landscape | Triple AI cliche combo | Name the specific shift |
| It's no secret that... | Then why are you saying it? | State the point directly |
| At the end of the day | Filler phrase | Cut it or say "Ultimately" |
| The fact of the matter is | Pompous wind-up | Just state the fact |
| Moving forward | Vague direction | Name the specific next step |
| With that being said | Unnecessary transition | Use "But" or cut entirely |
| It goes without saying | Then don't say it | Cut entirely |
| Without further ado | Filler ceremony | Cut and just begin |
| At its core | AI padding before the actual point | Cut - just make the point |
| Here's the thing... | AI stalling tactic | Cut - say the thing |
| The reality is... | Unnecessary wind-up | Cut - state the reality |
| When it comes to... | Filler transition | Cut - start with the subject |
| In order to... | Wordy | Just say "to" |
| It's worth noting that | If it's worth noting, just note it | Cut - say the thing directly |
| The question becomes... | Dramatic filler | Ask the question directly |
| Make no mistake | Melodramatic | Cut entirely |
| Look no further | Infomercial energy | Cut entirely |
| As a matter of fact | Wordy for "actually" | Cut or say "actually" |
| Needless to say | Then don't say it | Cut entirely |

### 9.6 Banned Openers (Never Use)

The first sentence AI writes is almost always the worst. These are the dead giveaways.

| Banned Opener | Why It's Banned | Use Instead |
|---|---|---|
| Let's dive in | Overused opener | Start with your first point |
| Let's delve into | Even worse version of dive in | Start with your first point |
| Let's unpack this | Trendy but hollow | Start explaining |
| Are you struggling with...? | Generic pain-point opener | Name a specific situation |
| Have you ever wondered...? | Reads like a bad infomercial | Share a fact or story instead |
| Picture this: | AI storytelling crutch | Just tell the story |

### 9.7 Banned Conclusions & CTAs (Never Use)

How AI ends content is just as predictable as how it starts.

| Banned Phrase | Why It's Banned | Use Instead |
|---|---|---|
| In conclusion | Reads like a school essay | End with a takeaway or action |
| To sum up / To summarize | Lazy closing | End with your strongest point |
| Key takeaways | Overused structure | "What to do next" or "The one thing to remember" |
| The bottom line is... | AI wrap-up cliche | State your final point directly |
| Final thoughts | Generic heading | End with a specific action or bold claim |
| What are your thoughts? | Generic engagement bait | Ask a specific, provocative question |
| Let me know in the comments | Low-effort CTA | Give a specific choice or action |
| Agree? | Weak engagement hook | Pose a genuine debate point |
| Don't miss out | Spammy urgency | Explain what they'll gain |
| Ready to transform your...? | Combines two banned words | Name the specific next step |
| Here's what matters... | Dramatic wind-up to the close | Just say the thing that matters |
